## Capture flow: shot from above, whole dish in frame

Calorie estimates need the whole plate in view, so every photo the user "takes" in the
prototype is top-down, with the whole dish centred inside the scanner ring. These frames were
composed for the ring:
- **Scale:** the dish fills about 85 % of the ring's diameter.
- **Edges:** where the original frame was too small, it is extended with a blurred copy of the same photo, which reads like a shallow depth of field.
- **Edge cases:**
  - E01 is intentionally blurred and too close;
  - E03 is darkened to 24 % brightness.
